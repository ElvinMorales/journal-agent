Guided intake artifacts map across Prompts and interfaces, Knowledge and resources, Memory, State, Planning and orchestration, Guardrails and governance, Outputs and schemas, Evaluation and observability, and Learning and iteration. The private-vault package, local MCP proposal workflow, and connector onboarding docs map to Tools, Knowledge and resources, Prompts and interfaces, Memory, State, Planning and orchestration, Guardrails and governance, Evaluation and observability, Runtime and deployment, and Learning and iteration. These artifacts add no taxonomy bucket. The taxonomy remains exactly the original 14 buckets. Live connector configuration, hosted runtime, tunnel implementation, and viewer work remain separately scoped.

The proposal fixtures and Journal Mirror walkthroughs provide inspectable synthetic paths through Evaluation and observability. They support manual boundary regression; they are not private traces, clinical validation, or runtime telemetry.

## Progressive Disclosure

Keep always-on artifacts short. Load skills only when their descriptions match the task. Load references and docs only when deeper context is needed.

For the complete repo-specific mapping and source-of-truth boundary, see `docs/taxonomy-mapping.md`.
