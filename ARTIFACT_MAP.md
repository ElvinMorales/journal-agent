Guided intake artifacts map across Prompts and interfaces, Knowledge and resources, Memory, State, Planning and orchestration, Guardrails and governance, Outputs and schemas, Evaluation and observability, and Learning and iteration. The private-vault package, local MCP proposal workflow, connector onboarding docs, local viewer, runtime regression suite, manual safety evals, and validation checklist map across the existing buckets without adding one. The taxonomy remains exactly the original 14 buckets. Live connector configuration, hosted runtime, tunnel implementation, and final release readiness remain separately scoped.

The proposal fixtures and Journal Mirror walkthroughs provide inspectable synthetic paths through Evaluation and observability. They support manual boundary regression; they are not private traces, clinical validation, or runtime telemetry.

## Progressive Disclosure

Keep always-on artifacts short. Load skills only when their descriptions match the task. Load references and docs only when deeper context is needed.

For the complete repo-specific mapping and source-of-truth boundary, see `docs/taxonomy-mapping.md`.
